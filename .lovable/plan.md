

## Conversion - Paid Ads Agency Landing Page

A dark-themed, single-page marketing website for a paid ads agency called "Conversion", recreated exactly as shown in the reference design.

### Sections (top to bottom):

1. **Navigation Bar** — Logo "Conversion" on left, nav links (About Us, Results, Services, Process, AOx), red "Book a call" CTA button on right. Dark background, sticky.

2. **Hero Section** — Stacked avatar group with "200+ businesses scaled" text, large headline "Ready to *scale* your brand with paid ads?" with italic on "scale", subtitle text, red "Book a call" button + white outline "Learn More" button.

3. **Partners/Trust Bar** — "You're in good hands" label, logos for Shopify Partners, Marketing Partner, Google Partner, Meta Business Partner on dark background.

4. **Why Us / Comparison Section** — Badge "Consultation", headline "But, why would you want to work *with us*?" with italic. Two-column comparison: left card "Other Agencies" with X-marked items (greyed out), right card "Conversion" with check-marked items (highlighted with red/orange accent).

5. **FAQ Accordion** — Two expandable questions: "How do you tailor Google Ads strategies for different businesses?" and "What's unique about your Meta Ads approach?"

6. **Portfolio / Creative Excellence Section** — Badge "Our Work", headline "Creative *Excellence*" with italic, subtitle. Grid of 8 portfolio/creative images (2 rows × 4 columns) with varied sizes, dark background.

7. **CTA / Final Section** — Badge "Built for real brands", large bold headline "READY TO OUTGROW YOUR CATEGORY?", descriptive paragraph, red "Start Scaling Today" button + "View Our Process" link. Right side shows a scheduling widget mockup with stats (brands served, growth campaigns, global efforts).

8. **Footer** — Logo "Conversion" + copyright, links (Privacy Policy, Terms of Service, License, Contact), social media icons.

### Design System
- **Background**: Very dark (near black) throughout
- **Primary accent**: Red/coral (#E74C3C style) for CTAs and highlights
- **Typography**: Large bold headlines, italic emphasis on key words
- **Cards**: Subtle dark elevated backgrounds with rounded corners
- **Overall feel**: Premium, dark, modern agency aesthetic
